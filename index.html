About

Analytics with a business-first mindset.

I'm a data analyst focused on turning raw business data into useful information using Power BI, SQL, Python and Excel — across cleaning, analysis, reporting and dashboard development.

I focus on what the numbers mean for the business: Time, Money and Risk. The goal isn't to create attractive charts — it's to answer a real question and make the result understandable to non-technical decision-makers.

Before analytics, I spent four years as a freelance writer for finance and business clients, which is where I learned the part most analysts skip: explaining a finding so clearly that someone acts on it without a follow-up meeting. I'm currently finishing my Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300) certification and looking for a remote analyst role.
