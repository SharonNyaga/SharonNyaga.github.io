About

Analytics with a business-first mindset.

I'm a Data Analyst focused on turning raw business data into useful information using Power BI, SQL, Python and Advanced Excel across data cleaning, analysis, reporting and dashboard development.

I focus on what the numbers mean for the business: Time, Money and Risk. The goal is not simply to create attractive charts, but to answer a real business question and make the result understandable to decision-makers.

Before moving into analytics, I worked as a freelance writer for finance and business clients. That experience strengthened my research, communication and remote-work skills — especially the ability to explain complex information clearly and work independently against deadlines.

I am currently preparing for the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300) certification while building an analytics portfolio focused on practical business problems.
