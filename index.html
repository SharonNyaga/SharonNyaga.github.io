Harbourline Finance: Safer Loan Approvals

A new approval rule that cuts estimated loan losses by two-thirds, from KSh 1.27M to KSh 0.41M, while still approving two in three applicants.

  • Reviewed 11,582 past loans and 3,000 new applications, correcting impossible ages, inconsistent work histories and other errors before any analysis began.
  • Built a risk score that predicts which borrowers are likely to fall behind, and proved it ranks risk more accurately than the credit bureau score the lender was relying on.
  • Compared four approval rules side by side so management can see exactly what each one means for risk, approvals and lending volume.
  • Showed which customer groups each rule affects most, and set out a controlled pilot so the change can be rolled out safely.

Wekeza Fintech: Payments, Lending & County Performance

Found that USSD, the dial-code menu used on basic phones, carries half of the KSh 356M moved through the platform.

  • Brought together customer, agent, county, transaction, loan, product and repayment records into one reliable reporting model.
  • Fixed a date mismatch that was quietly dropping transactions from trend reports, so monthly figures could be trusted.
  • Delivered a six-page Power BI report that lets a manager move from the headline numbers down to a single county or a single customer’s loan history.
  • Recommended tracking USSD reliability and costs closely, judging loan products on late payments rather than volume alone, and weighing county investment against population and agent coverage.
